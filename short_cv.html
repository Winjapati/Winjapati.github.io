Andrew Byrd — Historical Linguistics · Conlangs · Computation

Download Full CV (PDF)

Profile

My primary research focus is the creation and application of innovative approaches to solve difficult problems in historical linguistics.

I also work to educate the public about Proto-Indo-European (PIE) and historical linguistics through constructed languages and other forms of outreach.

Positions

  • Associate Professor of Linguistics, University of Kentucky, 2017-present
  • Assistant Professor of Linguistics, University of Kentucky, 2014-2017
  • Lecturer of Linguistics, University of Kentucky, 2011-2014

Education

  • PhD, Indo-European Studies, University of California, Los Angeles, 2010
    Dissertation: “Reconstructing Indo-European Syllabification” (advisor: H. Craig Melchert). 2nd place, Best Dissertation of 2011 in Indo-European, Indogermanische Gesellschaft.
  • Visiting Student, Linguistics, Harvard University, 2003–2004
  • BA, Linguistics (minors: Latin, German, Italian), University of Georgia, 2002
    Thesis: “The Phonology of the Laryngeals” (advisor: Jared S. Klein). Summa Cum Laude, with Honors.

Current Projects

  • DERBi PIE — a searchable database of Proto-Indo-European (Database of Etymological Roots Beginning in PIE), integrating multiple etymological reference works and search functions.
  • Five Novellas in Proto-Indo-European (with Brenna Reinhart Byrd) — immersive stories in PIE introducing the public to Proto-Indo-European language, culture, and archaeology.
