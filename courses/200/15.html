Going Beyond a Single Syllable

My conlang's syllable types are: CV/CVO/CVA/V/VO/VA

Give me every possible sequence of these syllable types as
one-, two-, and three-syllable words. There are also W segments (nasal vowels).

Rules:
- No VV (no two vowels may be adjacent)
- Only one W per word

Output a single slash-separated list with no spaces, like this:
CV/CVO/CVA/V/VO/VA/CVCV/CVCVO/...

Use code to generate the list, which I will copy to use in the Langua Gen website.
