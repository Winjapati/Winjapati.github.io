The Assignment

You’ll finish the website you started on Monday, November 16: a public, three-page academic website at https://your-username.github.io, with a homepage, a CV, and a research page. Your research page is where your final project will live in December.

You may build it in one of two ways:

  • Path A: a Quarto website, built with help from an AI tool.
  • Path B: HTML and CSS by hand, as on Monday and Wednesday.

Both paths are worth the same, and are graded on the same rubric.

My own website is your model. Browse it online, in HTML (Path B) or Quarto (Path A), and download the files: byrd_site_hw8.zip and byrd_quarto.zip. Its research page has the Pokorny project I’ve modeled for you in Components 1–3. Use it as a reference for what each page needs, but your site should be about you. (I’m a professor, so my CV has different sections from yours.)

On AI: For this homework only, Path A lets an AI tool write a first draft of your site’s files. Everything on your site still has to be true and in your own words: an AI doesn’t know your degree, your courses, or your project, and it will make things up. You must be able to explain every file in your repo. Path B is to be done without AI, as with every other homework. Either way, say how you used AI in your README.md.

Everything here is public. Use your UK email, and no phone number or home address.

What Your Site Needs

Every Page

  • The same navigation bar, with working links to Home, CV, and Research
  • Your own style, carried over from Wednesday: the UK blue header (or Quarto navbar) and your borrowed rules, each with a comment saying where it came from

Homepage

  • Your name, and a short introduction in your own words: your year, your major, and what interests you about language
  • A picture, with alt text (of you, or of something that represents you)
  • Contact information, with an email link
  • At least one link to another website

CV

  • Sections for Education, Experience, Skills, and Coursework
  • Experience can be jobs, volunteering, research, or campus organizations
  • Coursework includes LIN 301 and at least two other courses
  • At least one numbered list or table

Research

Your LIN 301 final project, as it stands now:

  • The project’s title, and your research question
  • Your dataset, with a link to where it came from
  • A table of some of your preliminary results from Component 3
  • A paragraph explaining what the table shows

Today’s Techniques

At least two of these three, on any page, and working:

  • An embedded <iframe> (a video, a map…) that’s relevant to the page
  • A grid of cards, using display: grid
  • A media query that changes your layout on narrow screens

Your README.md

Replace the text in the README.md GitHub made for your repo with a short section, How I Built This Site, that says:

  • how you used AI, if at all: what it wrote, and what you changed

Your Toolkit

HTML

  • <!DOCTYPE html>, <html>, <head>, <body>, <title>
  • <meta charset="UTF-8">, <meta name="viewport" ...>, <link rel="stylesheet" href="...">
  • <header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <div>, <footer>
  • <h1>–<h3>, <p>, <strong>, <em>, <br>
  • <ul>, <ol>, <li>, <table>, <tr>, <th>, <td>
  • <a href="...">, mailto:, <img src="..." alt="...">, <iframe src="..." title="...">
  • class="...", <!-- comments -->

CSS

  • selector { property: value; }, /* comments */
  • selectors: h2, .card, nav a, :hover
  • color, background-color, font-family, font-size, font-weight, text-align, text-decoration
  • padding, margin, border, border-radius, width, max-width, aspect-ratio
  • display: flex, flex-direction, gap, align-items
  • display: grid, grid-template-columns, fr
  • @media (max-width: ...px) { ... }

Quarto (Path A)

  • _quarto.yml: project:, type: website, output-dir: docs, website:, navbar:, href:, text:, format:, theme:, css:
  • Markdown: ## Heading, **bold**, *italic*, - list item, 1. numbered item, tables, [text](https://...), ![](picture.jpg){fig-alt="..."}
  • ::: {.class} … ::: (a <div> with a class)
  • raw HTML (like an <iframe>) inside a .qmd
  • quarto preview, quarto render, .nojekyll

Git and GitHub Pages

  • git add ., git commit -m "...", git push
  • Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main → / (root) (Path B) or /docs (Path A)
  • Ctrl + Shift + R (Mac: Cmd + Shift + R)
