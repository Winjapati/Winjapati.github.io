About Me

My research is about finding new ways to solve hard problems in historical linguistics, more and more often with computers. I especially work on Proto-Indo-European (PIE), the reconstructed ancestor of most of the languages of Europe, Iran, and northern India.

I also try to bring PIE to the public, through constructed languages for games and TV, translations, and interviews.

What I Work On

DERBi PIE

A searchable database of PIE roots, built from several etymological dictionaries.

Immersive PIE

With Brenna Byrd: a textbook and four novellas that teach PIE through stories.

Conlangs

Languages for games like Far Cry Primal and Flintlock: The Siege of Dawn.
